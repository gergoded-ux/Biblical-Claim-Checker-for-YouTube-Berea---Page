The Discernment Process

Four scholarly steps to scriptural discernment, executed instantly.

1. Transcribe

Instantly reads the YouTube video transcript as you watch.

2. Extract

Identifies checkable theological claims and timestamps them.

3. Retrieve

Finds relevant Scripture using 100% offline BM25 and semantic search.

4. Discern

Compares claims against Scripture, providing a structured, neutral report.
